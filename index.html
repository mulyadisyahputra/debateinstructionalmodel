RESEARCH-BASED INSTRUCTIONAL MODEL

DEBATE INSTRUCTIONAL MODEL

An Adaptation of Parliamentary Debate for 4Cs-Oriented Classroom Learning

A practical, structured, and student-centered instructional model designed to transform classroom debate into meaningful learning for Critical Thinking, Creativity, Communication, and Collaboration.

Debate Instructional Model was developed with the support of the Ministry of Higher Education, Science, and Technology of the Republic of Indonesia through the 2025–2026 Research and Community Service Program.

THE LEARNING FOCUS

Four connected competencies. One classroom model.

CRITICAL THINKING

Analyze arguments, evaluate evidence, identify weaknesses, and construct reasoned responses.

CREATIVITY

Develop alternative perspectives, arguments, solutions, and strategic responses.

COMMUNICATION

Express ideas clearly, respond to questions, rebut arguments, and communicate within time constraints.

COLLABORATION

Work strategically within teams, conduct research, support speakers, assess peers, and reflect collectively.

ABOUT DIM

WHAT IS THE DEBATE INSTRUCTIONAL MODEL?

DIM adapts the structured strengths of Parliamentary Debate into a classroom instructional model that supports meaningful participation, learning scaffolding, integrated assessment, and 4Cs development.

  1. 01 — Adapt parliamentary debate for school learning contexts.
  2. 02 — Integrate the four Cs into one instructional model.
  3. 03 — Create student-centered and interactive learning.

Structured Format

Clear debate procedures, role distribution, and classroom-friendly rules.

Role Rotation

Students experience different learning roles, not only speaking roles.

Effective Time Management

Structured allocation supports realistic classroom implementation.

Comprehensive Assessment

Assessment captures learning processes and 4Cs development.

PARTICIPATION BY DESIGN

EVERY STUDENT HAS A ROLE

DIM responds to unequal participation and passive non-speaking students through systematic role division, rotation, analysis, collaboration, and reflection.

Every role is designed to make participation visible, purposeful, and shared.

Main Speakers
Research Supporters
Assessment Panel
Time Keeper
Reporter / Notetaker
Audience Analyst

RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT

Designed through a structured development process.

DIM was developed through needs analysis, model development, expert appraisal, revision, classroom practicality testing, and refinement of supporting materials and tools.
